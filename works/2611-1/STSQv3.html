Labels & Parameters

Controls

Play button
Play / Stop · Hold or Shift+Click while playing: restart from step 1
Master Level
Drag slider: adjust volume
Knobs
Drag up / down or Scroll: adjust value · Arrow keys: adjust focused knob
Top row
Left to right: Pitch1 · Decay1 · Pitch2 · Decay2 · Grain · Length · Scale · Tempo
LEDs
Show the current step
Switches
Click: enable / disable each step
Bottom row
Adjust pitch for each of the 8 steps · Arrow keys / Scroll: one scale degree · Lowest setting: rest; low notes may be below hearing range
Menu button
Show / Hide Panel
Esc key
Close Panel
