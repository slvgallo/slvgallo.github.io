About

slvgallo is nothing more than a shady layabout who picks through the trash of a dying world and slaps together sound, image, and object with a shrug of "well, this'll turn into something, probably... eh, whatever." Deliberately smudging the line between order and chance, body and sign, reality and afterimage, the work refuses completion and drifts forever unfinished. Even the artist doesn't seem to fully understand the process; it's little more than a pathetic ritual to confirm one's own existence. Distort what you've scavenged, loop it, hurl it. All of it just traces the vague memory that "something must have been here." No deep insight, no message. Just a cold, ill-tempered experimenter watching the audience's sense of self slowly dissolve until they're left standing there, stunned. In the end, all he ever proves is that "there was nothing here." The audience walks back into their daily lives with a small hole left in the back of their heads; that's about the extent of the achievement. Whoever touches it gets quietly worn away, bit by bit. That's all there is to it. Well, that's about the size of it. slvgalloは、死にかけの世界のゴミを漁りながら「これでなんかになるかな…まあいいか」と適当に音と映像とオブジェクトをくっつける、ただの胡散臭い暇人である。 秩序と偶然、身体と記号、現実と残像の狭間で意味を意図的にずらし、作品は完成を拒んで永遠に未完のまま漂う。本人すらよくわかっていないこの作業は、自分の存在を確認するための情けない儀式に過ぎない。 拾ったものを歪め、ループさせ、投げ飛ばす。すべては「ここに何かあったはず」という曖昧な記憶をなぞるだけの行為だ。深い洞察もメッセージもない。ただ観客が自我を少しずつ溶かされ、立ち尽くす様子を冷ややかに眺めている、性根の悪い実験者である。 結局、彼が証明しているのは「何もなかったこと」だけ。観客は作品の後、頭の隅に小さな穴を残したまま日常に戻る。それがせいぜいの成果だ。触れた者は、静かに何かを削り取られていく。 それだけのことだ。 まあ、そんな程度のものだ。
